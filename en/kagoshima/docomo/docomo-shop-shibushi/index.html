01 / PREPARATION

What to Prepare Before Applying

  • Identity Verification Document (Residence Card / Passport)
  • Rakuten ID and Password
  • Credit card, Debit card, or JP bank account details for payment
  • Current phone number and contract owner name
  • Compatible device and SIM lock status check

What to check with your current carrier

Check your current account contract details, ID, and PIN to make application smoother.

If you want to keep your carrier email, check mail porting service terms and deadlines on your official carrier site.

02 / MNP

Things to Know About MNP Before Visiting a Store

Online Application

MNP One-Stop

When switching online between eligible carriers, you can complete the transfer directly from the application screen without obtaining an MNP reservation number.

In-Store / Manual

Using MNP Reservation Number

Depending on your contract or application method, an MNP reservation number may be required. Please apply promptly as reservation numbers expire.

※ Once your Rakuten Mobile line is activated via MNP, your previous carrier line will generally be cancelled automatically. Remaining device installments may continue.

03 / STEPS

How to switch from docomo to Rakuten Mobile

  1. 1

    Log in to Referral Campaign

    Open referral link and log in with your Rakuten ID before applying. Check latest conditions.

  2. 2

    Apply for Rakuten Mobile

    Select 'Transfer from another carrier (MNP)' to keep your phone number and enter subscriber info.

  3. 3

    Proceed with MNP Transfer

    Follow screen instructions using MNP One-Stop or reservation number.

  4. 4

    Receive SIM & Activate

    Set up SIM card or eSIM and verify signal switching and calling.

  5. 5

    Complete Campaign Terms

    Make a 10s+ call on Rakuten Link app within the deadline to fulfill point bonus terms!
