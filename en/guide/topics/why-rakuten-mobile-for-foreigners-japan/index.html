RAKUTEN MOBILE IN JAPAN

Why Rakuten Mobile Can Work Well for Foreign Residents in Japan

The appeal is not only the price. It can make monthly costs easier to predict, keep your Japanese number, and provide basic data when traveling abroad.

Open the employee referral offer first →Read the guide ↓

Quick answer

  1. 01Eligible MNP transfers through the employee referral may receive up to 14,000 limited-time Rakuten Points.
  2. 02Domestic data costs up to ¥3,278 per month even after 20GB.
  3. 03The plan includes 2GB of high-speed roaming each month in 100+ supported countries and regions.
  4. 04Rakuten Link offers free calls to most Japanese mobile and landline numbers, with exclusions.
  5. 05If online signup is difficult, you can look for a nearby Rakuten Mobile shop.

START HERE

Open the employee referral link before applying

To avoid missing the offer, begin with the employee referral link—not a separate application page.

  1. Open the Rakuten employee referral offer.
  2. Sign in with your Rakuten ID, or create one if needed.
  3. Apply for Rakuten Mobile using the same Rakuten ID.
  4. Check and complete the current campaign conditions.

The official FAQ states that simply applying from a normal page does not activate this employee referral. Referral login through the URL sent by a Rakuten employee is required.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

Predictable costs in Japan

  • ✓
    Up to 3GB costs ¥1,078, over 3GB to 20GB costs ¥2,178, and over 20GB costs ¥3,278 per month, tax included.
  • ✓
    With MNP, you can keep your current Japanese phone number after completing the transfer.
  • ✓
    Check coverage at home, work and along your usual routes before switching.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

What the referral offer means

  • ✓
    Open the employee referral link before applying and use the same Rakuten ID throughout.
  • ✓
    Up to 14,000 points applies to eligible MNP cases under the current employee referral conditions. Points are limited-time Rakuten Points, not cash.
  • ✓
    Campaign amounts, deadlines and eligibility can change, so rely on the campaign page shown after referral login.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

Useful when traveling abroad

  • ✓
    The plan includes 2GB of high-speed overseas roaming per month in 100+ supported countries and regions.
  • ✓
    This is useful for maps, messages and ride-hailing just after arrival, but it is not unlimited overseas data.
  • ✓
    Activate Rakuten Link in Japan and check overseas roaming settings before departure.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

Support when Japanese procedures feel difficult

  • ✓
    This site explains important Japanese buttons and terms in your language.
  • ✓
    If identity verification fails repeatedly, check name, address, document validity and image quality.
  • ✓
    A shop can guide the process, but support in a particular foreign language is not guaranteed.
