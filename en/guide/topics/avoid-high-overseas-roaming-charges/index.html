How to Avoid High Overseas Data and Call Charges

Before traveling, check the destination, included roaming allowance and how Rakuten Link behaves abroad.

CHECKLIST

Key points

  • ✓Confirm whether your destination is covered and how much high-speed roaming data is included.
  • ✓Use Wi-Fi and data-saving settings, and disable mobile data roaming when you do not need it.
  • ✓International calls can be charged differently depending on location, destination and calling app.
  • ✓Check usage in my Rakuten Mobile and consider an overseas data add-on before exceeding the allowance.
