Can You Pay Rakuten Mobile Bills with Rakuten Points?

Eligible Rakuten Points can be applied to monthly charges by configuring point usage in my Rakuten Mobile.

Key points

  • ✓Keep a valid credit card, debit card or bank account registered even when using points.
