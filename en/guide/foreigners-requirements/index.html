Basic Checklist

4 Core Requirements

  1. 01Medium-to-long-term residency in Japan
  2. 02Valid physical Residence Card (Zairyu Card)
  3. 03Registered Japanese address (Dorm/Apartment)
  4. 04Credit card, Debit card, or JP bank account

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Essential Documents for Application

Prepare these 3 documents before starting your application online or visiting a shop.

01

Residence Card (Zairyu Card)

Most Important: Mandatory for identity verification. Must be valid with photo, showing unexpired status, name, DOB, and visa category.

02

Passport

Frequently requested alongside your Residence Card to verify exact alphabetical name spelling and nationality confirmation.

03

Registered Address Proof

Must have an official registered address in Japan (Dormitory, share house, apartment). Hotels/Airbnbs are not accepted.

Visa Breakdown

Eligibility by Visa Type

  • ✓Student Visa: Very high approval rate; ideal for international students.
  • ✓Work Visa: Sign up under exact same conditions as Japanese citizens.
  • ✓Working Holiday: Fully eligible! Choose plan according to stay duration.
  • ✗Tourist Visa: NOT Eligible (Must use short-term travel eSIM/SIM).

Payment & Store Rules

Accepted Payments & Shops

・No Cash Allowed: Credit card, Debit card, or JP bank account (direct debit) is required.

・Online vs Shop: In-store staff verify physical documents. Online signup requires uploading photos via eKYC.

・Japanese Language Support: If you are worried about Japanese, visiting a physical Rakuten Mobile shop with POCKETALK is recommended.

TROUBLESHOOTING

4 Common Mistakes to Avoid

Prevent application rejection by double-checking these common pitfalls.

Mistake #1

Applying Before Residence Card

You cannot sign up before arriving in Japan or before receiving your physical Zairyu Card at airport immigration.

Mistake #2

Tourist Visa Misconception

Assuming a short-term tourist visa allows standard SIM contracts. Tourist visas do not issue Residence Cards.

Mistake #3 & #4

Unregistered Address / Cash

Attempting to pay with cash or applying before registering your address at the local city hall (Ward Office).
