FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch without visiting a store?＋

Yes, you can apply and activate completely online. In-person support is also available at Rakuten Mobile Shops.

Do I need an MNP reservation number?＋

For online MNP One-Stop applications, reservation numbers may not be needed.

Can I keep my current phone number?＋

Yes, select MNP transfer when applying to keep your current number.

What happens to my remaining device balance?＋

Remaining installments may continue with your old carrier even after switching.

When should I click the referral link?＋

Open the referral link and log in with your Rakuten ID before starting your application.
